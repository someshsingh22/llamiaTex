\section{Positioning vs.\ Latent-Space Work}
\label{sec:app_related_distinction}

\Cref{tab:related_distinction} expands the Related Work discussion. The closest prior work either studies communication among homogeneous language models (LLM-to-LLM) or converts a non-language agent's output back into text before the LLM consumes it. LLAMIA differs in internalizing a \emph{heterogeneous}, non-language agent's processed latent state directly into the LLM's reasoning trace.
