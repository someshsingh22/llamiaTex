\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\paragraph{Limitations.}
Our evidence is drawn from chess, where agent representations are well-characterized by interpretability work and evaluation is tractable. A complete multi-task evaluation beyond chess remains future work. The bottleneck claim is established for the Lc0 architecture family; whether it holds for fundamentally different agent representations (e.g., Stockfish NNUE, vision-language reward models) remains open. LLAMIA requires access to the agent's internal activations, which precludes application to closed-source agents without an intermediary. We do not evaluate whether the verbalization debt persists, shrinks, or grows at scales beyond 14B; the bottleneck claim is bounded by the scale range we test.
