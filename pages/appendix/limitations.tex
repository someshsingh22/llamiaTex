\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our evidence is drawn primarily from chess, where agent representations are well-characterized by interpretability work and evaluation is tractable, with initial cross-domain results on Go (\cref{sec:app_go}). A complete multi-task evaluation beyond chess remains future work. The bottleneck claim is established for the Lc0 architecture family; whether it holds for fundamentally different agent representations (e.g., Stockfish NNUE, vision-language reward models) remains open. LLAMIA requires access to the agent's internal activations, which precludes application to closed-source agents without an intermediary.
